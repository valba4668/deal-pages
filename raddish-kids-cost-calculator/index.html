How to use this calculator

Plan: pick monthly, 6 months, or 12 months. Prepaid plans are charged once, upfront. Months: only the monthly plan asks this, how long you expect to keep it (1 to 36). Sibling bundle: adds an extra culinary tool and apron patch each month for $5 per month per sibling, paid upfront. 3FREE: appears only for the 12-month plan and takes $68.85 off the upfront price (you pay for 9 of the 12 months); new customers only, first term only.

Limits: the calculator models US list prices only. It does not include taxes, gift pricing, one-off single-kit shop purchases, or the extra shipping fee Raddish charges outside the US. Ingredients are not included in the kits; Raddish emails a shopping list before each kit ships.

Privacy: everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere or stored.

Common questions

How much does Raddish cost?

$28.95 month to month, $155.70 upfront for 6 months ($25.95 per box), or $275.40 upfront for 12 months ($22.95 per box), per Raddish's plan page and checkout on November 26, 2026.

Which plans include the free apron?

The 6 and 12 month memberships include a free bonus apron. The monthly plan does not.

What ages is Raddish for?

Raddish says its clubs are for kids ages 4 to 14+. Younger kids cook alongside an adult; older kids can work more independently.

Can I cancel?

You can turn off the next auto-renewal at any time from your account. On a prepaid 6 or 12 month plan you keep receiving kits until the end of the prepaid period; the plan then renews unless you turned renewal off.
